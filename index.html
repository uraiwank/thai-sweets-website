Our story

From my mom's shop in Nong Khai to your table

Hi, I'm Nina, and Thai Sweets is my passion and my connection to home. I grew up in Nong Khai, Thailand, learning to cook in my mom's food shop alongside my grandmas. Today I handcraft authentic Thai desserts, snacks and cakes from my home kitchen in Vlaardingen.
