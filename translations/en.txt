Our mission & history
Sharing the flavours of Thailand, one bite at a time
Handcrafting authentic Thai treats at Thai Sweets in Vlaardingen since 2019, and bringing joy with every order.
Nina as a child with her mother in Thailand
Hi, I'm Nina
Thai Sweets is my passion and my connection to home. I grew up in Nong Khai, Thailand, learning to cook in my mom's food shop alongside my grandmas. After training at Suan Dusit International Culinary School, I moved to Vlaardingen and started handcrafting authentic Thai desserts, snacks and cakes from my home kitchen.
Making these Thai treats isn't just my business: it's my way of staying connected to home and sharing that connection with you. Whether it's a sweet dessert, a savoury snack or a special cake, each order means the world to me. Thank you for supporting my small business and letting me bring a little taste of Thailand into your life.

How to order
Made fresh, just for you
Everything is made fresh to order, so I need <strong>at least 1 week's notice</strong> before your pickup or delivery date.
Browse our menu
Take a look at Thai Sweets desserts, snacks and cakes to find what you'd like.
Place your order
Order online with your details and the items you'd like. Remember: at least 1 week's notice.
Confirm your order
I'll contact you by email or WhatsApp to confirm availability, the final price (including any delivery fee) and pickup or delivery.
Payment details follow after confirmation.
Enjoy your treats
Pick up your order or have it delivered on your chosen date. Everything is made fresh just for you.
Delivery & pickup
Delivery areas & minimums
Vlaardingen & Schiedam: regular delivery, minimum order €50.
Other nearby areas: delivery may be possible depending on date, time and location. Please ask.
Delivery cost
Delivery fees depend on distance. I'll confirm the exact cost when you place your order.

Pick up your order in Vlaardingen (Gretha Hofstralaan) at no extra cost.
